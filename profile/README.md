# AIHACKBins

**Open Knowledge. Stronger Security.**

AIHACKBins is an independent, open-source, community-driven knowledge base for **hacking AI systems** and **hacking with AI**. Our goal is to make AI security research accessible, practical, reproducible, and open to scrutiny.

Our philosophy draws inspiration from **Kerckhoffs's principle**: security should rely on resilient design and effective controls, not on keeping a system's workings obscure. Openness does not mean disclosing private data or credentials.

## Two areas, one community

### Hacking AI
Research into the security of AI applications and their components, including prompt injection, jailbreaking, agents, MCP, retrieval-augmented generation, model APIs, multimodal inputs, and supply chains.

### Hacking with AI
Reproducible ways to use AI in authorised security assessments: reconnaissance analysis, source-code review, API testing, vulnerability validation, security automation, and reporting.

## Our commitments

- **Free and open knowledge.** No paywalls or exclusive research tiers.
- **Practical research.** Clear prerequisites, examples, observations, and limitations.
- **Evidence first.** Separate experimental claims from verified findings, including negative results.
- **Attribution.** Credit researchers and respect upstream licences.
- **Collaboration.** Invite questions, corrections, research, and review from anyone.
- **Vendor neutrality.** Evaluate techniques and tools on evidence, not sponsorship.
