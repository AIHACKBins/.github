# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

AIHACKBins welcomes participants regardless of experience, background, identity or affiliation. We aim for a respectful, technically rigorous environment.

## Expected behaviour

- Discuss evidence and technical claims constructively.
- Give credit for ideas, code and research.
- Welcome questions and good-faith corrections.
- Respect privacy and avoid disclosing personal information.
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, threats or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information without consent.
- Deliberate misrepresentation of results or authorship.
- Spam, intimidation or repeated disruptive conduct.

## Reporting

For now, contact an organisation owner privately using GitHub's available contact mechanisms or request a private reporting channel without sharing sensitive details in public. A dedicated community contact address will be added once established.

Maintainers review concerns impartially, limit access to reports and take proportionate action, including warnings, removal of content or temporary or permanent restrictions. Participants directly involved in a complaint should not be its sole decision makers.

This policy applies to AIHACKBins repositories, discussions, pull requests and other official community spaces.
