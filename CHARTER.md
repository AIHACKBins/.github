# AIHACKBins Community Charter

**Version:** 0.1 (founding charter)

## Mission

AIHACKBins is an independent, freely accessible, community-maintained reference for AI security research. It covers two distinct areas:

1. **Hacking AI:** Understanding and testing the security of AI systems, agents, model integrations and supporting infrastructure.
2. **Hacking with AI:** Using AI responsibly to support penetration testing, vulnerability research and security engineering.

## Philosophy

Inspired by Kerckhoffs's principle, we believe strong security should withstand public scrutiny of its design and known attack methods. Public knowledge, reproducible methods and open critique can improve security. This does not require publishing private information, access credentials, or sensitive details of unresolved third-party findings.

## Principles

1. **Open access:** Core published knowledge is available without registration or payment.
2. **Open development:** Public issues, pull requests, and recorded decisions are the default.
3. **Reproducibility:** Claims describe prerequisites, testing conditions, observations and limitations.
4. **Transparent uncertainty:** Experimental, unverified, disproven and historical findings are clearly labelled.
5. **Attribution:** Researchers, contributors and upstream projects receive credit.
6. **Vendor neutrality:** Research is assessed on its technical merits.
7. **Respectful participation:** Review ideas and evidence, not personal characteristics.
8. **Sustainable stewardship:** Governance and review responsibilities should become shared as the project grows.

## Publication and disclosure

General security methods may be discussed openly. Contributions must not expose third-party secrets or personal data. Newly discovered vulnerabilities that could harm affected parties should follow appropriate coordinated disclosure before publishing sensitive details.

## Amendment process

Propose charter changes through a public issue and pull request. During the founding phase, maintainers document the decision and reasoning publicly. Governance can be revised as additional maintainers join.
