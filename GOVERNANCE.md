# Governance

**Status:** Founding model, version 0.1

AIHACKBins begins as a founder-led open-source project with an explicit goal of shared maintainership.

## Roles

- **Community members:** Anyone may read, discuss, report issues and propose changes.
- **Contributors:** People who submit accepted documentation, research, reviews or code.
- **Maintainers:** Trusted reviewers who triage issues, review pull requests and uphold project standards.
- **Organisation owners:** Administrators responsible for organisation settings, repository permissions and continuity.

## Decisions

Routine changes are discussed in issues and pull requests. Maintainers seek consensus; when consensus is unavailable during the founding phase, an organisation owner makes and documents the decision. Major changes to mission, licences, governance or project structure require a public proposal and an opportunity for comment.

## Becoming a maintainer

Sustained high-quality contributions, constructive review and demonstrated reliability are the main criteria. A current organisation owner appoints additional maintainers after discussing the nomination with existing maintainers. The appointment is announced publicly where appropriate.

## Removing permissions

Privileges may be adjusted for inactivity, compromised accounts, conflicts of interest or conduct violations. Sensitive security matters may require private handling, but public-facing policy outcomes should be explained when safe.

## Independence and conflicts of interest

Maintainers disclose material conflicts of interest related to submissions under review. Sponsorship must not buy editorial control or preferential validation.

## Continuity

When the team is established, maintain at least two trusted organisation owners with strong account security, documented access recovery practices and clear handover procedures. Do not share personal account credentials.

## Changes

Propose amendments via pull request. Revisit this governance document as the community grows.
