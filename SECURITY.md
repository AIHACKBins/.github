# Security Policy

## What to report

Please report security vulnerabilities in AIHACKBins-owned repositories, workflows, publishing systems or infrastructure privately rather than posting working exploits or secrets in public issues.

## Reporting channel

Until a dedicated address or private vulnerability reporting feature is configured, contact an AIHACKBins organisation owner to arrange a private report. Do not include exploit details or secrets in an initial public message.

We will acknowledge reports as capacity allows, investigate, and coordinate remediation and disclosure with reporters. No fixed service-level response time is promised during the founding phase.

## Scope

This policy concerns AIHACKBins infrastructure. Security vulnerabilities in unrelated AI products or services should be reported to the affected vendor through its disclosure process.

## Protecting research data

Never commit access tokens, credentials, third-party private datasets or personal information to public repositories.
